This patch release refines the recently introduced 'Convenience Switching' feature, specifically for setups where Roon is involved.

Roon doesn't only stream in its native RAAT format—it can also act as a transport for AirPlay and Squeezelite, and it can serve as a frontend for HQPlayer. In these combinations, RoPieee needs to know that Roon and the other service are actually working together; otherwise Convenience Switching would assume they're competing for the device and immediately stop the stream.

To fix this, a new switch called **'Controlled by Roon'** has been added to the AirPlay, Squeezelite and HQPlayer services. Enable it whenever that service is being driven through Roon, and Convenience Switching will correctly treat them as a single, cooperating source instead of stopping playback.

Next to this, a small improvement has been made to mDNS discovery on RoPieee Spinner.

Existing RoPieee users will automatically receive this update on their devices. For the changelog, images, and documentation, visit [RoPieee's GitHub page](https://github.com/RoPieee/RoPieee).
